# Scout

Sales intelligence over **USAC E-Rate** open data.

Who is buying, who is winning, what is expiring, what is open for bid — from free, structured, daily-refreshed USAC data. Built so a sales engineer can prep a call without a paid data vendor.

## What it covers

- Form 470s, FRN awards, vendors, and billed-entity profiles
- Category 2 five-year budgets, contract expirations, refresh prospects
- Per-rep pipeline, map of open 470s, Teams and email alerts
- States: Michigan, Indiana, Illinois, Ohio, Kentucky, Wisconsin, New York, New Jersey
